Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 14, 2026

SubKit ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use the SubKit mobile application ("App").

By using the App, you agree to this Privacy Policy.

1 Information We Collect

Personal Data

SubKit does not require you to create an account and does not collect personal information such as your name, email address, or phone number.

All subscription data you add (such as services, costs, billing cycles, and notes) is stored locally on your device and is not sent to our servers.

Usage & Diagnostics

We use Google Firebase services (Analytics, Crashlytics, and Performance Monitoring) to collect anonymous usage and crash data. This helps us improve app performance and stability.

This data may include:

  • Device type and operating system
  • App crashes and error logs
  • General feature usage

This information does not directly identify you.

Purchases

SubKit uses RevenueCat to manage subscriptions and in-app purchases.

  • Payment information is handled securely by the Apple App Store or Google Play Store
  • We do not store or process payment details
  • We only receive subscription status information to unlock premium features

Optional Google Drive Backup

If you enable the cloud backup feature:

  • SubKit accesses only the backup files it creates in your Google Drive
  • The app cannot access or view other files in your account
  • Backup data remains in your Google Drive and is not stored on our servers

2 How We Use Information

We use information only to:

  • Operate and improve the App
  • Manage subscriptions
  • Fix bugs and crashes
  • Respond to support requests if you contact us

3 Data Storage & Security

  • Your data is stored locally on your device
  • Backup files are encrypted before export
  • You remain in full control of your data and backups

4 Third-Party Services

SubKit uses the following third-party services:

  • Google Firebase (analytics and crash reporting) - Privacy Policy
  • RevenueCat (subscription management) - Privacy Policy

These services follow their own privacy policies.

5 Children's Privacy

SubKit is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

6 Data Deletion

Uninstalling the App will delete all locally stored data. Any backups you created remain under your control.

7 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
